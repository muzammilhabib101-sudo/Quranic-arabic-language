## Integrity checks (run on every build of this data)

- Tanzil has exactly 6,236 verses.
- Every Quran.com word has a matching Corpus entry, and word counts agree for every verse.
- The KFGQPC text and the Tanzil text contain the same letters in every verse (marks and spelling variants ignored).
- Any failure is listed in `problems.txt`; it must be empty.

The site's credits page (phase 8) must show these attributions.
